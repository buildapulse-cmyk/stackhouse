Customer support

Questions? We're here to help.

How long does a website take?

A starter website usually takes 1–2 weeks. Bigger sites and apps take longer, and we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

How do I pay?

Choose a package above and click "Pay". You can pay with Mobile Money or a bank card through Paystack. You'll get a receipt by email.

Do I need to pay everything at once?

No. You pay just a 10% deposit to start, and the rest when your project is ready to go live.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every package comes with free support after launch. After that, our monthly Care plan keeps your site updated and fixed.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. If you want to edit your own content, we can build that in and show you how to use it.
